Product Description

Our Laser Protective Lens is designed to protect the internal optical components of fiber laser cutting and welding systems from contamination caused by dust, smoke, metal splashes, and debris. Manufactured from premium fused silica and coated with advanced anti-reflection coatings, our protective windows provide excellent laser transmission, superior durability, and long service life. Suitable for high-power fiber laser systems from 1KW to 30KW.
